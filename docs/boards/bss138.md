# BSS138 level shifter (Adafruit 757)

Adafruit 4-channel I2C-safe bi-directional logic level converter, product 757. Four BSS138 N-channel MOSFETs, each with a 10 kΩ pull-up on both sides. The low side faces the [XC2-XL](xc2xl.md) XC2C256, which is 3.3 V and whose I/O absolute maximum is 4.0 V. The high side faces 5 V TTL. There is no programmable part and no bitstream.

The circuit is one discrete MOSFET per line, from NXP application note AN10441. The gate of every BSS138 ties to the low-side rail. The source is the low-side signal and the drain is the high-side signal. A high on either side rises only to that side's rail. A low on either side pulls the other side down through the MOSFET. Direction is set by which side drives, so the same channel carries a CPLD output or a TTL output.

## XC2-XL UART

The heartbeat UART is on connector B, header J4, at 115200 8N1. Pins 140 and 142 are VIO2, 3.3 V. A 5 V TTL high on those pins exceeds the 4.0 V absolute maximum. The shifter keeps the CPLD side at 3.3 V and presents a 5 V high to TTL.

J4 is cabled to [Breadboard 1](dbb1.md) connector A2 with the verified end-reversed pair map (Digilent J4-5/6 → **PA35/PA36**; Digilent 39/40/37/38 → **PA1/PA2/PA3/PA4**). The full table is on the breadboard page. HV is the TTL board's 5 V rail. A2 silk **VV** is Digilent J4-2 (VU from the wall plug) and is not HV. A2 silk **VDD** is Digilent J4-3 (3.3 V). A2 silk **PA3** is Digilent J4-37 (I/O), not the rail.

| Shifter | A2 silk | Digilent J4 | XC2C256 | TTL |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LV | VDD (37) | 3, VDD33 | — | — |
| GND | GND (39) | 1, GND | — | GND |
| A1 | PA36 | 6, B6 | 140, `uart_tx` | — |
| B1 | — | — | — | RX |
| A2 | PA35 | 5, B5 | 142, `uart_rx` | — |
| B2 | — | — | — | TX |

Shifter channels A3, A4, B3, and B4 are free. The XC9572XL inputs are 5 V tolerant and land on J5, which is breadboard B2, so this shifter is for the XC2C256 signals on A2.

A 5 V TTL TX wired straight to PA35, with no shifter, still needs the divider on that pin: 2.2 kΩ from the TTL TX to PA35, and 3.3 kΩ from PA35 to silk GND.

## PCB, Gerber, and netlist

The GitHub repository publishes the Eagle schematic and the Eagle board file. Gerber and IPC netlist files were not in that set. The schematic netlist is the connectivity source: each channel is source to the A pin, drain to the B pin, gate to LV, and a 10 kΩ pull-up on both ends.

## Bring-up

This board is outside the FPGA stage order. `make stage` does not take `bss138`. Fit and program the XC2C256 first, then connect LV, GND, and the two UART channels. HV comes from the TTL supply. The heartbeat line is still `HELLO,xc2xl` on `HIL_SERIAL`.
